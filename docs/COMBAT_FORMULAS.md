# ⚔️ Chronicles of Aethelgard: Combat Formulas, Scaling & Economy Mathematics

## 1. Core Mitigation Formulas
- **Physical Armor Reduction**: $DR_{phys} = \frac{\text{Armor}}{\text{Armor} + 500}$
- **Elemental Resistances**: Multiplicative damage scaling $(1.0 - R_{elem})$ capped strictly at 75%.
- **Critical Strike Scaling**: Total Damage = Base Damage $\times$ Critical Multiplier.
